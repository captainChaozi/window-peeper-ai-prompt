# Window Peeper AI prompt notes

Notes for making the **Window Peeper** meme video (someone in an apartment window takes a burger bite, stares awkwardly, and the curtains close), either with [Window Peeper AI](https://windowpeeperai.pro/) or with the free candidate prompt in a video model you already use.

## Two routes
1. **Browser generator** – upload one authorised adult portrait; the result is a newly generated apartment scene, not a pasted face. Current request settings: one portrait, 14 seconds, 720p, 9:16, fixed soundtrack. One-time credit packs from 10 credits / 6.90 USD.
2. **Free prompt** – copy the [candidate prompt](https://windowpeeperai.pro/guides/window-peeper-ai-prompt) into your own model and adjust it. No account needed.

## Portrait requirements
- One consenting adult; never children, celebrities, or anyone who has not agreed.
- Facing forward or at a slight angle; large, sharp, evenly lit face.
- No sunglasses, masks or hair across the eyes; avoid groups and dark side profiles.
- JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 10 MB, at least 512 px on the short side, no extreme aspect ratio.

## Adapting the prompt
- Keep the beats in order: window, bite, stare, curtains.
- Set vertical 9:16 explicitly if your model defaults to landscape.
- If a beat disappears, shorten other descriptions instead of adding words.
