# Support requests: what to send and what not to send

How to contact [Window Peeper AI](https://windowpeeperai.pro/) support, summarised from the [about page](https://windowpeeperai.pro/about) and [guide](https://windowpeeperai.pro/guide) (checked 2026-11-02).

## Address

support@windowpeeperai.pro — for task review, billing, privacy, removal, copyright or a factual correction.

## Include

- The relevant page, **task** or **order reference**
- A short description of what happened and what you expected
- For a rejected photo: the error shown next to the upload

## Do not include

- Passwords
- Payment-card details
- Private photos

## Before writing in

| Situation | First step |
|---|---|
| Balance not updated after checkout | Wait and refresh. Returning from checkout is not payment confirmation; do not buy again. |
| Video seems missing | Open **My videos**. Refreshing finds the saved task rather than starting it again. |
| Uncertain submission | It needs a status review. Do not keep pressing Generate. |
| Technical failure | A confirmed technical failure returns the credits used. Cash refunds are handled separately. |
| Photo rejected locally | Check format (JPG/PNG/WebP), size (≤10 MB), short side ≥512 px, no extreme aspect ratio. |
